# Electron2D community code of conduct

## Our commitment

Electron2D welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, languages and levels of experience. Everyone should be able to ask questions, contribute and discuss technical decisions without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Assume good intent while listening to how your words affect others.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and leave room for corrections.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only material you have the right to publish.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, spam and repeated disruption are unacceptable. These standards apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other spaces maintained by this project, and when representing Electron2D publicly.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on the affected contribution to report a conduct concern. If the content is not accessible or the concern involves the repository maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Include links and the information needed to assess the concern; avoid publishing another person's private information in a public thread. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [private security channel](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, request a change in behavior, close or lock a conversation, or restrict participation. They should apply these rules consistently, consider context and severity, and explain moderation decisions where doing so respects privacy. A serious or repeated violation may result in a permanent participation ban.

Participants can request reconsideration through the reporting channel with new context or evidence. Reports do not guarantee a particular outcome or response time. Maintainers are subject to the same standards as other participants.
