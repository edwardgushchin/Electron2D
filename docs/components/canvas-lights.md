# Canvas lights and occlusion

Last updated: 2026-10-11

`Light` is an Entity family. `PointLight` places a centered borrowed texture mask using Offset and TextureScale; `DirectionalLight` uses its transformed direction and normalized Height. Visible enabled sources are captured per rendered viewport. Inclusive canvas-layer and item-Z ranges select receivers; reversed ranges select nothing. Point masks intersect each item's LightMask, while directional sources ignore receiving masks. ShadowItemCullMask independently selects visible contour placements.

`CanvasItemMaterial.LightMode` selects Normal, Unshaded or LightOnly. Normal combines the item's color with ambient canvas modulation and light contributions. Unshaded bypasses ambient modulation and lights. LightOnly retains the original color, bypasses ambient modulation and multiplies its alpha by accumulated light alpha. Add/Sub/Mix retain their distinct numeric light contract and execute in capture order. Canvas draw blending remains a separate operation.

`CanvasTexture` has a distinct borrowed RID and diffuse/normal/specular channels. The diffuse channel defines size/image/opacity queries; an empty resource has size 1×1 and renders white. Normal XY channels determine a positive reconstructed Z; texture reflection/transpose and the drawing basis affect normal orientation. Specular map color/alpha, SpecularColor and SpecularShininess feed the fixed reflection equation. Filter/repeat override the drawing item's resolved values. Channels are borrowed, observed once when shared, copied through resource graph duplication and persisted by typed archives. Assignments reject disposed resources and cyclic views. Atlas tile padding retains all channels in source-owned transient resources.

`OccluderPolygon` copies finite point arrays and exposes closed/open edges and directed-edge culling. Its RID is borrowed until disposal. `LightOccluder` places a borrowed contour; shadow membership and SDFCollision are independent. TileSet occlusion layers default to light membership one and SDF participation false. TileData stores plural borrowed polygon slots, observes source changes/disposal and caches all seven transformed variants. Cached copies preserve closedness, culling and winding. TileMapLayer.OcclusionEnabled affects atlas contours; ordinary scene-tile children retain their own node policy.

The GPU backend evaluates light masks, normals, specular reflection and ray/edge shadows in the fragment pass. None/PCF5/PCF13 use one/five/thirteen visibility samples, with screen-pixel spacing controlled by ShadowFilterSmooth. This direct-contour sampling is the current backend adaptation rather than a quantized shadow-atlas implementation. Point-mask images share a retained floating-point atlas; texel-center clamping isolates neighbors. Storage and vertex uploads share a retained buffer. Preparation may allocate; stable replay reuses resources.

Shaders can request renderer-owned `SDF_TEXTURE` and float4 `SDF_COORDS`. Sampling coordinates are `framebuffer_position * SDF_COORDS.xy + SDF_COORDS.zw`. The texture's red channel is nearest participating-contour distance in framebuffer pixels, negative inside any closed contour and positive outside; open contours have unsigned distance. Internal edges of overlapping contours remain distance features. The field is generated only when a shader samples it. Viewport SDFOversize and SDFScale change area and resolution, while distance units remain pixels. These names are excluded from material parameters and archives. They project the current HLSL/GLSL shader profile, not an additional source language.

Static RenderingServer light/occluder/contour/canvas-texture operations retain caller-owned identities and borrow inputs. FreeRID/shutdown dispose owned placements/resources and preserve borrowed inputs. Retained placement interpolation captures both ends of scene physics ticks; idle transform assignments and resets remove stale history. Snapshot rebasing leaves canonical transforms unchanged. Scene lights and occluders retain inherited Entity interpolation.

Compatibility renders diffuse textures and Unshaded fixed materials, and rejects an actual light pass or programmable SDF shader before drawing. Programmable fragment materials receiving scene lights currently fail explicitly: automatic composition needs the shader profile's executable lighting interface. EditorOnly remains unexposed until editor scene rendering/hint ownership exists. Native axes and combined mask atlas axes are limited to 16384; unsupported floating-point targets fail explicitly. Light/shadow cost grows with pixels, sources and contour edges, and SDF generation with pixels and edges.

`LightingTests` verifies resource graphs, validation, copies and typed scene metadata. Native checks exercise actual GPU lighting/channel/SDF/retained-RID pixels, physics interpolation and compatibility rejection. Prepared owner-frame allocation measurements are distinct from driver/internal native allocations, foreign-platform verification and owner acceptance.
