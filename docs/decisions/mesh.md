## ADR 0092: Typed 2D mesh surfaces on the retained canvas

### Status

Accepted. Refines the typed resource projection in [ADR 0001](product.md#adr-0001) and the backend contract in [ADR 0028](rendering.md#adr-0028).

### Decision

Mesh : Resource supplies the applicable surface-query/material callbacks. ArrayMesh stores copied two-dimensional vertices, vertex colors, primary UVs and optional indices through MeshSurfaceData; named fields replace the heterogeneous array-of-arrays without Variant/object dispatch. MeshInstance : Entity projects the MeshInstance2D scene role, preserving borrowed Mesh/Texture, texture_changed and inherited canvas behavior. CanvasItem.DrawMesh and logical RID drawing feed the same retained canvas submission; all five primitive topologies execute through native GPU and compatibility triangles. Points and lines keep one-framebuffer-pixel width after transforms, with no antialias fringe. Per-surface materials override the drawing item's material; unsupported shaders fail through the existing fallback gate.

The native/backend-neutral surface arrays preserve the exercised pinned channel packing: positions are little-endian float X/Y pairs; colors clamp/truncate to RGBA8 UNORM on import; primary UV remains two little-endian float components. Vertex stride is eight bytes. Attribute stride is four bytes for color plus eight bytes for UV when present. Region updates use byte offsets, including partial records, validate affected finite values before publishing and preserve untouched bytes. UseDynamicUpdate remains a policy hint and does not make other surfaces inert. Resource queries return independent channel copies; successful repeated updates and replay reuse prepared storage. This is not a public SDL buffer or GPU-device pointer.

Resource Mesh.GetRID is a stable weak borrowed logical identity until disposal, independent of an active renderer. RenderingServer owns caller-created mesh identities until FreeRID/shutdown; owned and resource-owned identities are distinguished before mutation/free. Retained draws observe current ArrayMesh buffers without requiring a fresh command. Custom Mesh callbacks publish Changed after edits and provide copied typed channels; their prepared replay snapshots are refreshed on that revision. Custom callbacks must keep the resource revision stable while preparing a snapshot; changing it rejects the mixed result and permits retry. Repeated replay uses the captured surface count without calling user callbacks. Custom callback allocation is separate from the runtime preparation measurement. MeshInstance uses the ordinary scene owner/capture mutation guards and local Resource graph duplication.

This profile does not redefine unsupported source capabilities as irrelevant. Morph targets/weights and LOD storage/selection require the first typed 2D deformation or scale-selection producer/consumer slice. Normal/tangent/UV2/custom channels require their typed storage, byte-format and actual shader-attribute consumer integration; general Mesh skin/bones/weights require typed MeshSurfaceData/packed-region channels and an actual Mesh palette consumer, over the now executable Skeleton/Bone and Polygon palette. Attribute compression requires its encoder/decoder and visible precision verification. Those inputs and flags reject or remain absent, with exact member-specific coverage triggers; no inert setter or nominal data-only support is reported as complete. GetFaces extracts copied local Vector2 triangle faces, expanding indexed and sequential triangles and alternating-winding strips; point and line surfaces contribute nothing. The returned TriangleMesh resource and lightmap-size hint remain excluded as 3D intersection/lightmap capabilities. Shadow mesh requires the first 2D light/shadow geometry consumer; placeholder meshes require the missing-asset placeholder producer. 3D-only triangle-mesh/collision/lightmap operations retain their accepted product exclusions, not an invented 3D runtime type.

ImmediateMesh : Mesh builds one invisible draft through SurfaceBegin, current Color/UV and SurfaceAddVertex2D, then SurfaceEnd publishes sequential ArrayMesh-backed storage. First attribute activation backfills earlier vertices with the assigned value; later assignments affect only future vertices. Empty/incomplete topology rejects without losing the draft. ClearSurfaces cancels the draft and removes completed surfaces. End, clear and inherited material edits publish Changed after commit and outside the storage gate, keeping Electron2D resource/canvas invalidation consistent even where the pinned immediate builder does not notify on clear/material edits. Duplication copies completed geometry and material graphs, never transient draft state. Methods serialize individual operations; callers synchronize whole construction sequences shared across threads. Structural authoring is a cold allocation boundary; replay and repeated material/transform updates use prepared storage. Secondary UV is absent until its storage and real shader consumer enter the first attribute slice; no nominal setter is added.

### Ownership, errors and verification

Meshes own channel storage and borrow materials; nodes and drawing commands borrow meshes/textures. Owned server meshes close with the renderer. Invalid geometry, nonfinite inputs, out-of-range indices and regions fail before mutation. Resource Changed runs after committed edits; callback failure does not roll back valid geometry. Warmed successful paths are checked under [ADR 0014](resources.md#adr-0014). Exact managed/native pixel and platform limits are recorded in the [mesh component](../components/meshes.md); builds do not establish rendered or human acceptance.

### Repeated mesh instances and 2D visibility

MultiMesh owns a fixed 2D schema: eight transform floats followed by optional four-float instance color and raw shader data. The 2D/3D format selector is implicit and absent. Channels must be configured at zero capacity. Resizing clears current/previous records; equal capacity preserves them and shrinking clamps the visible prefix. Packed and legacy-array queries copy storage. Ordinary writes outside a physics tick reset the edited previous record; SetBufferInterpolated supplies a copied explicit pair. Fast interpolates components; High decomposes compatible nonsingular 2D bases into rotation/scale/skew and falls back to components for singular, reflected-sign changes or unrepresentable decomposition. Widened component blending preserves finite cancellation. This corrects the pinned High implementation's use of a 12-float 3D record against an eight-float 2D stride; that unsafe read is not preserved.

SceneTree captures prepared registered resources before physics callbacks and closes capture even when the setting changes during the tick. MultiMeshInstance refreshes the shared resource policy on entry and policy/reset notifications. Shared instances use the resource's most recently assigned effective policy; node-specific disabled presentation uses current poses. Reset copies current values into previous records. Resource duplication resets transient snapshots and renderer binding; copying stored state into an existing resource retains its active binding. Mesh/texture remain borrowed, and resource Changed callbacks invalidate attached canvas state safely from worker threads.

Mesh.GetAABB/OnGetAABB and MultiMesh bounds are applicable 2D visibility operations projected to Rect2, including XY bounds for every stored vertex/topology. No public 3D AABB is added. Automatic instance bounds merge the visible prefix's transformed mesh rectangles; presentation culling uses interpolated poses. CustomAABB overrides the resource-local rectangle, with the zero rectangle selecting automatic computation. The native viewport and managed scissor checks exercise culling. Custom geometry callbacks must retain stable resource revisions during bounds and surface preparation; recursive preparation, disposal and mixed snapshots reject before submission and permit retry.

DrawMultiMesh, MultiMeshInstance and server-owned logical identities expand visible geometry in surface-then-instance order into reusable canvas triangles. Compatible surfaces coalesce into shared native batches; this is CPU geometry expansion, not hardware instanced drawing or a writable GPU instance buffer. Both current native backends consume poses, colors, UVs, material overrides and inherited canvas policies. Custom data is preserved as raw float32 components on all backends and reaches an optional GPU fragment float4 at location two, independently of tint. Compatibility rejects custom shaders explicitly. Shader vertex programs remain their separately recorded dependency.

RenderingServer owns created MultiMesh storage and borrows its Mesh identity; resource-owned identities remain weak and cannot be mutated/freed through owned APIs. MeshCreate now records its canonical RID in the owned resource so linked mesh queries return that same identity. MultiMeshAllocateData executes the ordinary typed allocation atomically; useIndirect=true rejects before mutation. Indirect commands and the two RenderingDevice buffer RID getters remain blocked until actual writable instance/command buffers, device identity, synchronization and a draw consumer exist in the first hardware instance-buffer slice. No synthetic device RID is returned.

### Skeletal scene palettes and weighted polygons

Skeleton : Entity and Bone : Entity preserve the applicable scene/canvas inheritance and dimensionless family mapping under ADRs 0004/0008. Direct Bone chains form child-order DFS palettes, retaining inverse rest and authored versus temporary poses. Singular/unset rest remains authorable with identity deformation. BoneSetupChanged runs after commitment; structural changes during modifier execution reject before mixed application. A stable weak borrowed logical RID connects retained Polygon commands to the live rig independently of native startup; server FreeRID rejects scene ownership. Server-owned palette creation/attachment remains a separate concrete storage/consumer slice.

Polygon stores copied bone paths/weights and prepares four strongest positive influences, retaining equal-weight order and original contour source indices. It normalizes with widened arithmetic, maps actual interpolated Polygon/Skeleton coordinate bases and uses pose multiplied by inverse rest. TopLevel follows actual presentation pose. Missing/count-mismatched/zero-weight bindings preserve original vertices; inversion ignores skin. One viewport/CanvasLayer is required. Triangulation, UV/color/materials remain ordinary retained triangles shared by GPU/compatibility. General Mesh skin arrays/regions/eight-weight channels remain exact dependencies, not inert storage.

SkeletonModification/Stack/LookAt use typed resource callbacks, scene-owner guards, ordered nullable borrowed slots and exclusive live scene binding. Shared ProcessPhase projects semantic source Process/Physics modes explicitly (Idle=1/Physics=0); no duplicate enum is introduced. Physics stages overrides and restores authoring; Idle restores authored animation, executes, blends and consumes transient overrides. Persistent=true retains requests, including without a stack, correcting the pinned source's inverted consumption condition to its documented API. LookAt submits transient requests to keep disable/reset behavior, uses actual endpoint/target/constraint rotation and normalizes multi-turn angles beyond the pinned one-turn adjustment. ConstraintInLocalSpace is the typed stored coordinate policy; editor gizmos require the actual editor owner.

PackedScene force-copies a rig's stack/modification graph through the existing duplication session regardless of ordinary shallow resource policy, retaining graph aliases and instance-owned cleanup. Rest/endpoints, concrete settings and bounded versioned Polygon records persist; setup, weak scene targets, palette identities and overrides do not. Callback failures retain committed structure, attempt all pose restoration, release guards and permit explicit setup retry. Warm successful pose, ordinary redraw and replay reuse prepared storage under ADR 0014. Actual managed/native/fresh-process checks and limits are recorded in [skeletal animation](../components/skeletal-animation.md); other IK/physics/editor capabilities retain precise triggers.

Three concrete resources project the complete applicable TwoBoneIK/CCDIK/FABRIK family to SkeletonModificationTwoBoneIK, SkeletonModificationCCDIK and SkeletonModificationFABRIK, with fully uppercase acronyms across selectors/accessors. They retain semantic phase/strength and transient override ownership rather than a new solver/rendering interface. Relative indexed joint authoring is typed, bounded and copied; actual stored CCD constraint_in_localspace is available through explicit typed indexed methods. Bound index selections also author paths, retaining identity through hierarchy changes. Missing/degenerate selections skip undefined solves.

TwoBoneIK applies conservative scaled lengths and finite inner/outer/coincident reach handling. CCD preserves its single authored-order pass; FABRIK keeps ten iterations and .01 pixel tolerance, measures scratch convergence, uses finite coincident direction fallback and positive reflected length. Final target rotation and non-root world magnets participate in its actual solve. Nonuniform/skewed bases keep the source minimum-scale length policy with no exact anisotropic reach guarantee. The existing scene transaction now restores pre-pass requests as well as authored poses on failure, using reusable setup buffers, so partial IK results cannot escape a failed pass. Joint configuration persists through existing exact resource schemas/independent stack copies; weak caches and scratch do not. Actual headless/fresh-process/native evidence and remaining physics/editor/mesh limits are recorded in the component.

Jiggle and StackHolder retain the same typed resource/pose profile as SkeletonModificationJiggle and SkeletonModificationStackHolder. Jiggle configuration/default propagation and transient finite force/velocity history are distinct from scene Bone nodes. Reset seeds every valid origin including index zero; positive mass prevents undefined zero division. Current global defaults seed new slots. Collider mode uses the existing world-scoped body ray in Physics; Idle collider use rejects explicitly instead of an inert collision setting. The query protects a dynamic point, not the whole bone/mesh. Individual successful joint state commits remain observable after a later failure, while scene poses and prior requests retain the established transaction restoration.

Held stacks borrow the same skeleton, keep independent strength and phase selection, and release weak incoming-holder leases only after their final holder disappears (with direct-root binding tracked separately). Copies always duplicate child graphs through the shared alias-preserving resource session. Setter/setup/execution cycle/depth guards and pre-transition active disposal/reset guards bound failure; assignment commits before setup and failed child setup invalidates the parent for retry. Explicit child execution starts a transaction for that child rather than entering unrelated root siblings. These refinements preserve actual applicable resource ownership and do not introduce a public generic solver/backend interface. Exact storage, native/headless checks and limits are documented in the component.

### Physics-driven scene bones

PhysicalBone : RigidBody preserves inherited mass/material/forces/contact/disable/Freeze ownership and selects a separate Bone through a Skeleton/PhysicalBone parent chain. Dimensionless BoneIndex/BoneNodePath apply across the entire public family. Requested simulation and actual valid attached activation remain distinct. Inactive bodies follow their bone using static noncolliding backend fixtures, with configured public filters retained; follow-while-simulating aligns at processing boundaries but retains real rigid forces/contacts. Existing authored direct Joint children are borrowed: auto configuration assigns physical parent/body endpoints and aligns origins; a Skeleton parent preserves authored endpoints. No joint or shape is synthesized, and ADR 0084 anchor sampling remains authoritative. Physics unit-scale/zero-skew restrictions remain in force.

SkeletonModificationPhysicalBones consumes solved body global poses at its selected phase, submitting local transient overrides with ordinary stack strength and rollback. Default Idle observes completed physics; explicit Physics stages prior solved poses. Copied bounded paths and exact physical-node names control real bodies; breadth-first fetch omits nested foreign rigs. Pending detached commands apply once at setup and remain transient. Exact factories and forced scene stack copies retain authored node/resource/joint state through fresh-process archives.

These adaptations repair pinned source holes rather than preserve inert behavior: bone2d_nodepath actually resolves selection, initially false simulators are noncolliding followers, following continues after stop, parent-first Ready alignment avoids shifting already seeded nested bodies, valid index boundaries reject safely, and -1 explicitly clears numeric selection. Body transition commits are independent of scene pose transaction rollback. Cold transitions may rebuild fixtures; prepared simulation/pose/Polygon replay uses retained storage under ADR 0014. Editor generation/gizmos, server palettes and general Mesh skin channels remain separate executable slices.
