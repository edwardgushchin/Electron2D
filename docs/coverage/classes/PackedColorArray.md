Implemented call sites project color sequences to `Color[]` or `ReadOnlySpan<Color>`; see [Gradient](Gradient.md) and [CanvasItem](CanvasItem.md). Their copying and ownership contracts are audited on those APIs. Binary conversion remains a separate row below.
